Municipal infrastructure · Hydraulic modeling

Municipal water distribution model

WaterCAD layout and hydraulic checks for a proposed 41-node community network.

My work · water demand, network layout, and WaterCAD hydraulic analysis

The challenge

For a proposed Missouri community, I needed a water network that could serve different land uses and maintain pressure under average and peak conditions. The project required a demand calculation, physical pipe layout, and hydraulic model.

How we approached it

I estimated demand for housing and community facilities, drew the distribution route through the site, and represented its 41 junctions, six-inch pipes, pump, reservoir, and storage tank in WaterCAD. I compared simulated junction pressures and pipe velocities under average and peak demand and prepared the report submitted for the course.

What the work showed

The proposed network met the modeled pressure range of about 40-96 psi, while low velocities in some branches flagged a circulation issue for further design. The report recommends reviewing pipe sizes and low-flow paths and considering flushing. These are course model results, not a constructed water system.
