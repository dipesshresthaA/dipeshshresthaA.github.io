Troy roadway and parking lot design

Civil 3D roadway design, intersection geometry, shared use path, and parking plans in Troy.

My design work · Civil 3D roadway and path; intersection analysis; AutoCAD parking plans

The challenge

The municipal design assignment called for an extension of Sr. Airman Bradley Smith Boulevard toward US 40 in Troy, Illinois, a shared use path and sidewalk connections, and a parking design for a church and an overflow lot. I needed to fit truck movements, sight distance, access, and pedestrian routes into one coherent plan.

What I did

I designed the roadway in Civil 3D, developed the horizontal and vertical geometry, selected a WB-55 design vehicle, checked turning radii and sight triangles, and prepared the typical section with 12-foot lanes and 6-foot sidewalks. I drew the shared use path and its connections at Collinsville Road, US 40, IL 162, the assisted living entrance, and the parking area. I then produced the AutoCAD parking layouts with accessible spaces, a pickup and drop-off area, service access, overflow parking, landscape islands, and crossings. I also prepared the calculations and report for the course submission.

What the work showed

The drawings connect roadway geometry, shared use path continuity, and parking circulation. They make the critical junctions, crossings, and accessible routes visible for review. The project was an academic proposal and was not constructed.
