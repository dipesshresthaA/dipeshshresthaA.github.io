Pavement construction life cycle study

Construction-stage life cycle comparison of asphalt and concrete alternatives.

My role · data collection, asphalt openLCA model, report and presentation; two-person team

The challenge

Choosing between asphalt and concrete by one environmental measure can hide tradeoffs. For our sustainable engineering study, the team used the same 23,545-square-meter reference area for two pavement alternatives on a representative two-mile road.

How we approached it

I collected input data and built the asphalt alternative in openLCA using hot-mix asphalt paving, aggregate base, energy, and truck transport. My teammate and I compared the asphalt and concrete construction inventories using TRACI 2.2, then prepared the report and presentation. The normalized chart gives the broad pattern; the acidification and global-warming exhibits show how the preferred alternative changes by impact category.

What the work showed

In this preliminary construction-stage model, asphalt was lower for global warming and several other categories, while concrete was lower for acidification, marine eutrophication, non-carcinogenic effects, and smog formation. The work taught me to state the system boundary before recommending an alternative: maintenance, use, and end-of-life stages were outside this comparison.

Selected work

The comparison excludes maintenance, rehabilitation, traffic use, and end-of-life stages. It does not establish a whole-life pavement winner.
