M.S. thesis · research in progress

Traffic signal structure failures across the U.S.

My thesis examines reported traffic signal structure failures across the United States. I search public Facebook posts and news articles, review each candidate incident, check duplicates, and record the location, reported cause, affected component, failure type, and consequences. The historical database covers reports from 2013 to 2026.

The difficult part is treating incomplete public reports carefully. I keep the two source types separate and distinguish what a report confirms from what it leaves unknown. The analysis is still underway, so I’m presenting my method rather than a final finding.
